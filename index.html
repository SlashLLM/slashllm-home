Featured case study

Impress AI · HR Tech
“
We needed every candidate evaluated to the same standard. Not approximately the same. Exactly the same.

The client needed to evaluate interviews at scale without sacrificing fairness or rigor. We delivered a real-AWS, computer-vision-powered analysis engine that does exactly that: asynchronously, accurately, and cost-tracked from day one.

The challenge

Recruiting teams needed to assess video interviews at scale, consistently across multiple competency dimensions, without the time cost or bias of manual review. Existing tools could not detect if a candidate was reading from off-screen notes; integrity scoring was effectively broken.

What we built

A production REST API combining AWS Bedrock (Claude Opus 4.6) for 7-dimensional NLP scoring, AWS Transcribe for real speech-to-text, and MediaPipe computer vision for frame-by-frame iris gaze tracking. Async S3 architecture enables unlimited concurrent interviews with full fault tolerance.
