Our story
The gap wasn't in ideas —it was in execution.

SlashLLM started with a frustration: too many companies spending months on AI "strategy" with nothing to show for it. Decks. Roadmaps. Proof-of-concepts that never left the sandbox.

The world had plenty of AI consultants who could tell you what to build. It had very few teams that could build it, deploy it, and make it work at scale.

So we built SlashLLM as the agency we wished existed: a small, fast, technical team that goes from prompt to production. No talent marketplace. No layers of project managers. Just builders who understand both the technology and the business problem — and who ship real systems into real production environments.

Today, we work with growth-stage startups, SMBs, and mid-market enterprises across SaaS, fintech, healthcare, and HR tech — helping them build AI products, deploy intelligent agents, and optimize their AI infrastructure.
